The passenger runs the map.

Live riders around you and across the world, convoys with chat and walkie-talkie, voice with riders in range, and routes with an ETA. The driver drives.

Riders stream in live, not on a delay.

Everyone in range shows up as their actual car in their actual paint. Positions arrive once a second and glide at 60 fps. Tap a car to see who is driving, wave, or invite them.

  • FiltersNearby, friends, radius, my road, in sight ahead.
  • FriendsAdd riders by username, see who is online, invite them to a convoy even when they are off your map.
  • GlobalA worldwide layer of every rider, clustered, and honestly marked about five minutes behind.

Form a convoy. Stay on the same page.

Name it, invite riders from the map or your friends list, and members get tethered to you with a live link line. Up to six riders, open or locked, join by code.

  • ChatGroup chat for the convoy, plus a rally point everyone can see on the map.
  • VoiceWalkie-talkie: push-to-talk, open mic, or toggle. Peer-to-peer WebRTC with a relay fallback, and never outside your convoy.
  • SOSOne button tells the whole convoy you have stopped and need help.

Routes, a garage, and a voice for the road.

The rest of the dashboard, all inside the same map.

Routes with a real ETA

Search any place or tap the map. Traffic-aware routing with distance, time and arrival, spoken turn-by-turn, and a chase camera that plays the trip at 1x, 5x or 20x. Tap a rider’s pin to route to where they are.

Your car is your marker

Year, make, model, paint, finish, body, engine, drive, horsepower, mods. Fourteen paint presets or any color. On everyone else's map you are your car, in your color.

Also on the map

  • Proximity voice: auto-link with riders in range, on your road, or in sight
  • Invisible mode when you want to ride unseen by anyone outside your convoy
  • Edge arrows that point at convoy riders off screen
  • A rally point and an SOS button for your convoy
  • Friends, waves and rider profiles
  • Block and report, right from any rider card

Make it yours

Eleven themes or build your own, six accent colors or any hex, five map styles, miles or kilometers, and the glass blur and animations switch off for older phones.

Built for the passenger, and only the passenger.

Every screen assumes someone else is driving. That is the condition the whole app rests on, and it is the first thing you agree to: three separate confirmations stand in front of every way in, guest or account.

Crash reports never include a location, a message or an account, and you can switch them off in Settings. Delete your account from the app, or from the website without installing anything.
