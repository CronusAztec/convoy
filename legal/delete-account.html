Delete your Convoy account

App: Convoy · Package: tech.odoreeci.convoy · Developer: [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]

You can delete your Convoy account yourself, at any time, without asking us and without giving a reason. It takes about ten seconds and it cannot be undone.

Delete it now

In the app — fastest

  1. Open Convoy and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar, top right → Profile.
  3. Open Settings (the gear icon) and scroll to Legal & data.
  4. Tap Delete account, confirm your password, and type DELETE to confirm.

Open the delete screen now → — opens Convoy and takes you straight there.

By email — if you cannot sign in

Write to privacy@odoreeci.tech from the email address on the account, and tell us the username you want deleted. We will verify that you control the account and delete it within 30 days, usually far sooner. If there is no email on the account, send us the username and your recovery code and we will do the same.

We will never ask you for your password by email.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold about the account is erased from our live systems immediately:

  • Your username, and your sign-in credentials — password hash, recovery code hash, and any linked social sign-in identity
  • Your email address, and any pending confirmation, reset, or sign-in links
  • Your profile — display name, handle, avatar, vibe
  • Your garage — every car detail you entered
  • Your friends and any pending friend requests, in both directions
  • Your performance stats and personal bests

You are dropped from live view the moment the account goes. Your username is released and someone else may take it.

What was never stored anyway

  • Your location history — never written to our database. Live positions sit in server memory for about five minutes and are discarded.
  • Your voice — walkie-talkie audio is never recorded.
  • Chat — held in memory for the life of a convoy, then dropped. Messages already delivered to other riders’ screens are theirs, and we cannot reach into their devices to remove them.

Backups

Encrypted database backups roll off within 30 days. After that, no copy of the account remains anywhere in our systems. Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Resend) delete on their own published schedules — see the Privacy Policy.

On your own device

Deleting the account does not clear what is cached on your phone or browser. To clear that too, open Convoy → Profile → Settings → Data → Reset app data, or uninstall the app.

Guest riders

If you rode as a guest, you never had an account with us and there is nothing here to delete. Everything lived in your browser’s local storage. Clear the site data, or uninstall the app, and it is gone.

Trouble?

privacy@odoreeci.tech — we answer within 30 days and usually within a couple of days.
