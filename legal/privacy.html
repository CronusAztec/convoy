Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version. Convoy is a live map. To put you on it we handle your precise location and broadcast it to other riders near you. If you make an account, we hold that account on our servers — your username, your profile, your car, your friends, and your email if you gave one — until you delete it. You can delete it yourself, at any time, from Profile → Account → Delete account, or at odoreeci.tech/delete-account. We do not sell your data and we do not run ads.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Location — the core of the Service

DataWhyLegal basis
Precise GPS position, heading, speed, accuracyTo draw you on the map, place you near other riders, run navigation, and time performance runsConsent — you grant the OS permission and can revoke it
Background location (Android, while a convoy or trip is active)To keep your position live with the screen off, via a foreground service with a visible notificationConsent

Who sees your location. While you are connected in live mode:

  • Riders within about 30 km of you receive your position in real time, roughly once a second, along with your display name, avatar and car.
  • Everyone else, worldwide, may see you on the delayed global layer — a snapshot at least five minutes old, clustered and coarse.
  • Members of your convoy see you continuously, at full rate.

Ghost Mode removes you from the feed for everyone outside your convoy, including the global layer.

We do not store location history. Positions live in server memory only, in a rolling buffer of roughly the last five minutes, and are discarded after that. Nothing about where you have been is written to our database. Trip logs, parking pins and performance runs stay on your own device; if you are signed in, only the result of a performance run — a time, never a route — is saved to your account.

2.3 Things you send to other people

  • Convoy chat — relayed through our server to convoy members. Held in memory for the life of the convoy; not written to the database.
  • Walkie-talkie voice — peer-to-peer between convoy members wherever possible. If a direct connection fails, audio is relayed live through our server. We do not record it and it is never written to disk.
  • Hazard reports and waves — broadcast to riders nearby with your rider identity attached.

Legal basis: contract. Anything you broadcast is seen by other people — treat it as public.

2.4 Technical data

  • IP address — processed transiently to route your connection, apply rate limits and block abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the Service available and safe.
  • Session tokens — a short-lived access token and a refresh token, held in your device’s local storage so that you stay signed in.
  • Server logs — account events such as sign-in, password change and email confirmation, for security and abuse investigation.

Convoy sets no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. It uses local storage for your own settings and session, which is strictly necessary for the Service to function.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data. Under the CCPA/CPRA we have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.
  • We do not serve advertising, and we share nothing with ad networks or data brokers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or track you across other apps and sites.
  • We do not record your voice, and we do not store your location history.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children — see §8.

8. Age

Convoy is not for children. You must be at least 16 to use it, or older where your country sets a higher age for consenting to data processing. Convoy is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to privacy@odoreeci.tech and we will delete the account.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Delete your account and everything in it — Profile → Account → Delete account in the app, or odoreeci.tech/delete-account. It is immediate and irreversible.
  • Get a copy of your data — email privacy@odoreeci.tech. We respond within 30 days.
  • Correct it — most of it is editable in Profile.
  • Withdraw location consent — revoke the permission in your OS settings. The map still works; you simply are not on it.
  • Go invisible without leaving — Ghost Mode.

If the UK or EU GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, including objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to your supervisory authority — in the UK, the ICO.

If you are a California resident you have the rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we will never discriminate against you for exercising a right.

10. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and WSS; the Android build refuses cleartext). Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as salted hashes, by our authentication provider — we cannot read them. Access tokens are short-lived. Rate limits and per-IP ceilings apply across the API and the realtime layer. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a flaw, please tell security@odoreeci.tech before you tell anyone else.

11. Changes

If we change this policy materially we will raise the version, change the effective date, and show you a notice in the app before the change takes effect for you. Continuing to use Convoy after that means you accept the new version.
